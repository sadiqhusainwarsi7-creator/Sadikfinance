FAQ

Common questions

Answers to questions we get asked most often about contacting Sadik Finance.

How quickly does Sadik Finance respond to emails?
We typically respond within 24–48 hours on business days. More complex inquiries — such as partnership proposals or technical issue investigations — may take slightly longer, but we read every message personally.
Can I request a specific trading topic to be covered?
Absolutely. Use the contact form and select "Suggest a Topic" as your subject. We review all suggestions and prioritise topics that benefit the wider community — such as underserved ICT concepts or frequently misunderstood SMC principles.
Does Sadik Finance offer affiliate or partnership programmes?
We are open to relevant partnerships that align with our educational mission. Send a partnership request via the contact form with details about your proposal, including the nature of the collaboration and how it serves our audience.
What should I include in a business inquiry?
Please include your full name, organisation (if applicable), a clear and concise description of your proposal, expected timelines, and any relevant links. The more context you provide, the faster we can give you a useful response without back-and-forth.
Is there a phone number or physical address for Sadik Finance?
No. Sadik Finance operates entirely online. Email is the only official contact channel — contact.sadikfinance@gmail.com. There is no phone number or physical address.
How do I report a bug or broken content on the site?
Select "Report an Issue" as your subject in the contact form and describe the problem clearly — including which page it occurred on, what you were trying to do, and what you expected to happen. Screenshots are extremely helpful.
