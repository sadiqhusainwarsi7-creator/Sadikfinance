Privacy Policy

How Sadik Finance collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our website, tools, and educational content.

01

Introduction

Sadik Finance ("we," "us," or "our") operates a trading and financial education website focused on ICT (Inner Circle Trader) and Smart Money Concepts. We are based in India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our tools, or interact with our content.

By using Sadik Finance, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms outlined here, please discontinue use of the website.

02

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: data you provide directly, and data collected automatically as you browse the website.

Information You Provide

  • Contact form submissions — name, email address, subject, and message content.
  • Newsletter sign-ups — email address.
  • Any correspondence sent directly to our email address.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data — pages visited, time spent on site, referring URLs, and click behaviour.
  • Device data — browser type, operating system, device type, and screen resolution.
  • Approximate location — inferred from IP address (country/region level only).
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies — see our Cookies Policy below.
03

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Sadik Finance website and its tools.
  • To respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form.
  • To send newsletter updates to subscribers who have opted in.
  • To analyse site traffic and user behaviour for performance improvements.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or fraudulent activity.
  • To display relevant advertising through third-party ad networks such as Google AdSense.

Note: we do not sell your personal information to third parties under any circumstances.

04

Cookies Policy

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and remember preferences. Sadik Finance uses cookies for the following purposes:

Cookie TypePurpose
EssentialRequired for core website functionality, such as navigation and security.
AnalyticsHelp us understand how visitors interact with the site (via Google Analytics).
AdvertisingUsed by Google AdSense to deliver relevant ads and measure ad performance.
PreferenceRemember settings such as theme or display preferences.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling certain cookies may affect site functionality.

05

Google Analytics Usage

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics collects information such as pages visited, session duration, device type, and general geographic location. This data is aggregated and anonymised where possible, and is used solely to improve site content and user experience.

Google Analytics operates under its own privacy policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, available directly from Google.

06

Google AdSense Usage

Sadik Finance may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings page. Third-party vendors, including Google, also use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website.

07

Affiliate Links Disclosure

Some links on Sadik Finance — including links to prop trading firms, brokers, or trading tools — may be affiliate links. If you click an affiliate link and make a purchase or sign up for a service, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

We only recommend products and services we believe provide genuine value to our audience. Affiliate relationships do not influence the educational content or opinions shared on this website.

08

Third-Party Services

Sadik Finance may use the following categories of third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Analytics providers (e.g. Google Analytics) for traffic and behaviour insights.
  • Advertising networks (e.g. Google AdSense) for displaying ads.
  • Email service providers for newsletter delivery and contact form processing.
  • Hosting and content delivery providers for serving website content reliably.
  • Affiliate partners for prop firm deals and trading-related services.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party service you interact with through our website.

09

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes secure data transmission practices and limited access to personal data.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

10

User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to deletion — request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal limitations.
  • Right to opt out — unsubscribe from newsletters or marketing communications at any time.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the Contact Information section below.

11

Children's Privacy

Sadik Finance is not directed at individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate action, including deletion of that data.

12

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or website functionality. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
