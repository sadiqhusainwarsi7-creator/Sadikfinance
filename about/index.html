Our Story

About Sadik Finance

Sadik Finance was founded in 2026 in India by Sadique Hussain Warsi with a simple goal — make high-quality trading education actually accessible, structured, and free of noise.

What started as a small collection of ICT and Smart Money Concepts notes has grown into a complete learning library, covering market structure, liquidity, confirmation tools, entry models, trading psychology, and the practical tools traders need to execute consistently — including a dedicated trading journal and free educational PDFs.

Every resource on this platform is built with the same standard: clear explanations, real examples, and no unnecessary jargon.

Our Mission

To build Sadik Finance into a global platform that provides high-quality trading, investing, and financial education resources for traders and investors worldwide.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted global destinations for trading education, financial resources, tools, and market knowledge.

The Why

Why Sadik Finance Was Created

Most trading education online is scattered — paid courses that gatekeep the basics, free content that's inconsistent in quality, and communities that recycle the same screenshots without ever explaining the reasoning behind them.

Sadik Finance was created to fix that gap: a single, structured platform where ICT and Smart Money Concepts are taught the same way every time, with dedicated guides for each concept and a clear path from theory to a working entry model.

The goal has never been to sell a "secret" strategy. It's to teach the actual logic behind price movement so traders can build their own consistent process.
